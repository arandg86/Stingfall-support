I bought the full game. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I can't hear the music.

Stingfall respects your iPhone's Silent switch. Turn Silent off, and check that Music is on in Settings → Audio.

How do I change the controls?

Go to Settings → Controls. You can switch between the Buttons and Gestures layouts, invert up and down, change how hard you turn, and move the speed control to the other side.

Can I turn off vibration?

Yes. Go to Settings → Audio → Haptics.

The game is stuck on the loading screen.

Close Stingfall completely (swipe it away from the app switcher) and open it again. If you see a Try again button with a message, please send us a screenshot of it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Stingfall is a game made by arandg ("we").

We do not collect any personal information. Stingfall has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device. The game saves your progress and settings (levels beaten, characters unlocked, music volume, control options) on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Purchases. The optional "Full game" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children. Stingfall does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
